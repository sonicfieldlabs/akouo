# /corpus

Audit the inherited listening conditions behind a present model, dataset, codec,
taxonomy, transcript system, or other trained apparatus.

## Chain

1. `akouo-router` inventories the actual evidence and forbids invented lineage.
2. `corpus-listening` records known, partial, unknown, and not-applicable corpus
   disclosures; inherited categories, exclusions, and defaults; and the relation
   between past training and the present encounter.
3. `transductive-media-listening` maps the technical chain that turns those
   inheritances into an available aperture.
4. `critical-political-listening` examines ownership, labor, permission,
   extraction, jurisdiction, and who can contest the inherited ear.

## Contract

- Unknown training provenance stays `unknown`; do not infer a dataset from model
  behavior, brand, output style, or provider.
- A model card or provider statement is attributable documentation, not proof of
  every training item.
- Corpus absence is not world absence.
- Corpus listening describes inherited conditions; it never grants authority to
  capture, retain, disclose, classify, or act on the present object.
- Emit `listening_provenance.corpus_lineage` and connect each statement to a
  declared `listening_sources` entry.

Recommended next mode: `critical-political-listening`.
